OUR UNIVERSITY FACES
THREE MAIN GAPS

Classical computers hit a wall — and that wall runs through our campus. Between our students and real quantum work: the machines, the method, the space.

01 · GAP ONE

HARDWARE LIMITS

Our lab PCs simply freeze on complex quantum code. A circuit the class should explore in minutes becomes a locked screen, a spinning cursor, and a lost session.

02 · GAP TWO

THEORY ONLY

We study quantum on whiteboards, but we lack hands-on experience with real industry tools. The equations are beautiful — the workflows are missing.

03 · GAP THREE

NO DEDICATED SPACE

Without a dedicated space, momentum dies between lectures. The curious lose their playground — and the ambitious take their talent elsewhere.
