# Quantum Simulation Lab — Landing Page

A lightweight, single-page landing site for our student-managed **Quantum Simulation Lab** seed proposal (2026).

Built with **pure HTML + CSS + a touch of vanilla JS** — no frameworks, no build step.

## The story
*"The Perfect Meal — A Story in Four Courses"* — a restaurant that must find one perfect plate among 1,000,000+ combinations is the perfect metaphor for what classical computers can't do… and what quantum computers do in minutes.

## Sections
- Hero with animated stats
- The Story (the perfect meal → the wall)
- 10,000 Years vs 3 Minutes
- The 3 Gaps (hardware · theory · space)
- The 3 Steps (clouds · hardware · software)
- Budget — $50,000, three jobs
- Goals · Why Invest · Team · Invest Form

## Run locally
Just open `index.html` in a browser — or serve it:

```bash
python3 -m http.server 8080
```

## Deploy
Deployed on **Vercel** — any push to `main` goes live.
